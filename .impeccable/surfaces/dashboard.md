## Direction contract
THESIS: Quota is a tide. It drains with use and refills on a schedule. The surface reads like a night chart and its almanac, not like a grid of KPI cards. It refuses the hero-number card grid and purple gradients.
OWN-WORLD: Chart-navy ground (oklch ~0.17 0.03 245) and depth bands from deep teal to pale shoal sand. "Drying" (exhausted) shows as hatched sand, hazards in chart magenta, and the live position in buoy amber. Hairline rules, no cards, no shadows. Bahnschrift with tabular numerals throughout and Consolas for the call log.
STORY: In about 3 seconds he sees which accounts are shoaling, when the next high water comes (reset), whether a turn is underway, and which combo step is carrying traffic.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A top almanac strip (gateway, live turns, calls/min, tokens today, next high water). Left 62%: the tide table (accounts × 5 windows, depth-band cells with % and reset), and under it the combo routes drawn as 4-step chains with hit counts. Right 38%: the soundings log (calls, with to-scale TTFT/total bars), the sessions/sub-agent tree, memory budget bars, and Dora "not connected".
FORM: Night nautical chart + tide almanac, my grounded candidate #3, seed: none (unavailable in brief; roll key not exposed).
SIGNATURE MOVE: Each quota cell is filled from the bottom like water in a tide gauge, banded by depth. A soft 1px wave line marks the waterline and eases when it changes.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
